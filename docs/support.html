Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up my OpenAI API key?

Go to Settings in the app and enter your OpenAI API key in the AI Configuration section. You can get an API key from platform.openai.com.

How do I create a learning path?

Tap the + button on the Home screen. Follow the step-by-step wizard to describe your goal, assess your level, set your time preferences, and generate your personalized path.

Can I adjust my learning path?

Yes! Open any learning path and tap "Adjust Path with AI" to provide feedback. The AI will modify future weeks based on your input while keeping completed milestones intact.

How does iCloud sync work?

Enable iCloud sync in Settings. Your learning paths will automatically sync across all your Apple devices signed into the same iCloud account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, find PathForge, and cancel. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Is my data private?

Yes. All learning data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, data is encrypted and stored in your iCloud. We never store your data on our servers. See our Privacy Policy.
