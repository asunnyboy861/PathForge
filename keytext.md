## Description

**Master any skill with AI-powered learning paths tailored just for you.**

PathForge creates personalized, step-by-step study plans based on your goals, current level, and available time. Whether you're learning to code, picking up a new language, or mastering a musical instrument, PathForge designs the perfect roadmap to get you there.

**KEY FEATURES:**

🎯 **Personalized Learning Paths**
Tell PathForge your goal, and our AI creates a detailed week-by-week plan with specific tasks, resources, and milestones. No more wondering what to study next.

📚 **Smart Task Management**
Each week includes curated tasks with estimated durations, resource links, and hands-on projects. Check off tasks as you complete them and watch your progress grow.

🔄 **AI Path Adjustment**
Stuck on a concept or moving faster than expected? Provide feedback and PathForge's AI will dynamically adjust your remaining plan while preserving your completed progress.

📊 **Progress Tracking & Statistics**
Visualize your learning journey with detailed charts, streak tracking, and milestone completion stats. Stay motivated by seeing how far you've come.

☁️ **iCloud Sync**
Your learning paths sync seamlessly across all your Apple devices. Start studying on your iPhone and continue on your iPad.

🔔 **Study Reminders**
Set daily study reminders to build consistent learning habits and maintain your streak.
